1. Overview and Commitment

Mindful Breath: Relax & Focus ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mindfulness and breathing training application developed to assist users with stress reduction, relaxation, and focus.

Your privacy is our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy details our strict commitment to zero personal data collection, zero network transmission, and total on-device privacy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Mindful Breath strictly collects no personal information:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Health or Biometric Records: We do not track, record, or store medical data, heart rate measurements, or microphone audio.
  • No Device Telemetry: We do not collect device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address), IP addresses, or location coordinates.
  • Local Storage Only: Any preferences you select (such as chosen breathing technique, sound volume, or session countdown) and your accumulated session statistics are stored solely on your device using Android's private SharedPreferences and are never transmitted outside your phone.

3. Device Permissions

Mindful Breath adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege. The application requires zero sensitive runtime permissions:

  • No Internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET is not requested).
  • No Location access (no GPS or network location).
  • No Storage or Media access (no photos, media, or file access).
  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • Optional Haptic Feedback: The app utilizes the device's internal vibration motor (android.permission.VIBRATE) purely for tactile breathing cues. This permission cannot access personal data.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Mindful Breath is 100% ad-free and tracker-free. We do not incorporate any third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or external behavioral analytics (such as Facebook SDK, Google AdMob, or Firebase Analytics).

5. Health and Wellness Disclaimer

Medical Notice: Mindful Breath is created solely for personal wellness, relaxation, and mindfulness training. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or a substitute for professional clinical medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding any medical conditions or severe respiratory issues.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Mindful Breath does not collect, process, or transmit any user data from any individual, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy. The application is safe for users of all ages.
